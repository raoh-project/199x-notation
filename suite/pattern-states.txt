# The count is held where it decides something: against the limit of 250000. A runner puts each
# pattern p of s states beside a filler, as the pattern (?:p)|a{0,n}, whose a{0,n} is n + 1 states.
# The choice is one state, each arm one more than itself and the whole pattern one more, so the
# pattern is 4 + s + n + 1 states. With n = 250000 - 5 - s it is at the limit and is read; with
# n = 250000 - 4 - s it is one past and is BEYOND, past MACHINE_STATES.
